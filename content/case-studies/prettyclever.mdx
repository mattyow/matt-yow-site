## Preface

PrettyClever is a more than a design studio. They partner and embed with startups to build their brand and product from the inside. Isabelle Bilmes reached out to simply fill the gap of needing a proper brand for the studio: *what does PrettyClever look like?* The spark nods to visual trends in AI while being a distinct mark. Lastly, a bit of art direction was provided on what social posts and web instances could look like.
