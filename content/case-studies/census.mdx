## Preface

My first task was establishing a cohesive and enduring brand for Census — there wasn’t really a “visual identity” when I started. Onwards I was in full charge of brand management and the complete brand experience.

My four year tenure involved Series B announcement; all physical media across global in-person events, brand experience, and swag; managing external partners and contractors (motion, animation, developers, engineers, designers); all ads, sales decks, and necessary marketing collateral; owning the website across multiple redesigns to align with updated messaging. 

In 2024, I was promoted to *Principal Brand Designer*.

On May 1, 2025, Fivetran acquired Census.
