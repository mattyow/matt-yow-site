## Prometheus, the Fire-bringer

Donny Flynn and I worked at Census together. When he founded his own company, it was a great match to work together again.

The first step was establishing the name and core brand strategy that would unlock everything else. We landed on Blaze being a Prometheus archetype, a fire-bringer: the combination of a Sage and Outlaw. But this comes with friction in the end-of-life and caregiving space. The resolution is in positioning, voice, tone, and delivery of messaging.
